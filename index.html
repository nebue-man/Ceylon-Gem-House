From the gravel to your hand, in five steps

01 / 05

Raised from the illam

Timber-braced pits, five to twenty-five metres deep. The gem gravel comes up by hand, and the land is filled back in when the work ends.

Washed in woven baskets

Swirled in running water until the clay is gone. What stays behind is read by eye, one pebble at a time.

A frosted blue crystal

Proceeds are shared among landowner, licence holder, pump owner, investors and miners, as they have been for generations.

Shaped by hand

Each facet is planned to keep the colour deep and the weight honest. Calibrated work is cut to a tenth of a millimetre.

Examined and sealed

At export, a state valuer and a Customs appraiser examine the parcel, and it is sealed in wax by three parties.
